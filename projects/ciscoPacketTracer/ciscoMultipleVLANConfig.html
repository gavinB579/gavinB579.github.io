Cisco Packet Tracer - Multiple VLANs Configuration

The focus of this submission was to configure multiple VLANs on a network topology. I utilized a lot of the skills from my previous submissions, and built onto it. I had to create multiple VLANs, all connected to the switch directly. The computers in each VLAN had to be able to connect locally, but not to computers in other VLANs. We also had to verify that the router can ping all of the computers, regardless of the VLAN they were apart of. To accomplish this, I had to utilize trunk ports, VLAN tagging, subnetting, and more.

Making the network topology and configuring the devices was a fun, albeit challenging, experience. Making the devices connect locally but not between VLANs, all while the router can ping all of the devices, was a difficult task, but I am happy with how it turned out. Like the other projects, I had help from my professor, but I still had to do a lot of my own research and problem solving, referencing notes and previous projects to get everything working.
